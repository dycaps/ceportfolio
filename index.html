About Me

I'm a licensed civil engineer with a strong foundation in structural engineering, quantity estimation, and construction documentation. I specialize in structural design, quantity takeoffs, BOQ preparation, and cost estimation for residential, commercial, and infrastructure projects.

My approach combines engineering principles with attention to detail, ensuring that every project is designed with safety, accuracy, efficiency, and constructability in mind. From structural analysis and design to detailed quantity takeoffs and estimates, I focus on delivering reliable results that support both sound engineering decisions and effective project planning.

Based in the Philippines, I bring a practical understanding of structural engineering and construction processes, with experience working with technical drawings, structural plans, quantity measurements, and project documentation.
